📐 Graph Paper Background Options

⭐ Recommended: Layered Dot Grid

Why: Subtle, professional, adds texture without distraction. The overlapping dot patterns create visual interest that references data/systems without being too literal.

Best for: Website background, dissertation figure backgrounds, video overlays

To use: Add class bg-layered or copy its CSS to your body tag
